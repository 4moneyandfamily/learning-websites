LESSON 00

First decide what the picker is for.

A model picker is a budget decision disguised as a dropdown. Every prompt you send has a cheapest model that can handle it and a most expensive model you do not need. This course is about always landing on the first one.

Strong fit

  • Routing prompts by input type: audio and video go to Omni-Flash, screenshots and cheap visual work go to Plus, deep reasoning goes to Max.
  • Saving real money by not using the flagship for jobs a cheaper model does better.
  • Matching the model to the job: your voice dumps, your YouTube verdicts, your dispute letters, your trading screenshots.
  • Knowing each model's hard limits before you trust its output.

Weak fit

  • Treating the picker as a quality ladder and always choosing the top name.
  • Expecting one model to do everything. None of these three does.
  • Pasting secrets, account numbers, or credentials into any of them.
  • Using a cloud model where your local Hermes on Ollama is the private path.

LESSON 03

Meet all three in nine minutes.

One prompt per model, three minutes each, each on something from your real life. The point is not the answers. The point is feeling which brain fits which job.

01

Omni-Flash: send it your voice

Record a two-minute voice note about your day, the way you already dictate voice dumps, and ask for a summary with timestamps. Or paste a YouTube link and ask for the key claims with timestamps. This is the model that hears.

02

Plus: send it a screenshot

Take a screenshot of a staffing agency application form, your trading screen, or any dense UI, and ask what it shows and what to fill in. Plus was built to read screens. Watch it name the fields.

03

Max: send it a hard question

Ask for the bull and bear case on a ticker, judged against your screen: institutional size, P/E gates, no parabolic runs. Or hand it a long document and ask for the decisions inside it. This is the scholar. Give it something worthy of the price.

✓

Success is observable

Three answers back. The voice note comes back with timestamps. The screenshot comes back with its fields named. The hard question comes back structured, with both sides argued. If a model fumbles its native job, the routing card is wrong, and you fix it now, not later.

04 · Core mechanics  ·  05 · Agentic mode  ·  06 · Automation
LESSON 06

Wire it up, with a fence.

API access turns these models into pipelines: your voice dumps transcribed for under a cent an hour, your videos summarized on arrival. Automate the boring parts. Keep the fence around everything that matters.

01

Pick one pipeline

Start with voice dumps to Omni-Flash. You already dictate them constantly. Record, send, get back a timestamped summary. One pipeline, learned well, beats three half-built ones.

02

Run it by hand three times

Before any API key exists, do the manual version three times and confirm the output is correct every time. Automation multiplies whatever the manual process does, including its mistakes.

03

Add the API only when it is boring

When the manual version is correct and dull, wire the API call. Function calling, web search, structured outputs, and context caching are all supported on the Omni-Flash API.

Automate when

  • It transcribes, summarizes, or extracts. Voice dumps, meeting notes, video verdicts.
  • The input and output are both well defined and you have watched it work by hand.
  • The worst failure is a bad summary you can regenerate.
  • It saves you the same ten minutes every single day.

Keep manual when

  • It sends, pays, trades, files, submits, or publishes anything.
  • It touches credentials, account numbers, or legal deadlines.
  • The output goes to another human without your review.
  • You cannot describe the trigger in one sentence.

LESSON 08

Cloud model rules.

These are someone else's computers. Treat them that way and they are safe, cheap, and useful. Treat them like your own machine and they will teach you the difference.

Where does my data go?

To Alibaba Cloud infrastructure when you use the API or the Qwen app. Some API routes offer US residency and zero data retention on pay-as-you-go plans. Check the route before sending anything sensitive, and assume the chat app keeps what you type.

What never goes into a prompt?

Passwords, API keys, full account or card numbers, and anything you would not want in a breach disclosure. Last-four masks are fine; full numbers are not. This is the same rule you already follow everywhere else.

Can I self-host any of these three?

No. Plus, Max, and Omni-Flash are API-only under closed commercial licenses. Alibaba promised Max weights the week of August 10, 2026, but verify a real repository on Hugging Face before believing it. Your private path remains local: Hermes on Ollama on your Windows box.

Do I trust the model's output?

No more than you trust any other AI in your setup. Your verifier-desk rule applies here too: another model proposes, you check it against your verified numbers. Benchmarks in this course are vendor-reported until independent labs reproduce them.

What about the realtime variant?

Qwen3.8-Omni-Flash-Realtime holds a live audio-visual connection. A live camera and microphone pointed at a cloud model is the highest-exposure way to use any of these. Use it deliberately, never by default.
